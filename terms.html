SECTION 06

6. Camera Analysis & Moderation Disclosure

To maintain transparent and accurate disclosures regarding our technology stack:

  • Client-Side Reaction Analysis: Facial smile, laugh, and blink detection is performed locally in your web browser via client-side face landmark analysis. Facial mesh coordinates are processed in temporary browser memory to calculate game scores and are not transmitted to a storage server.
  • Real-Time Video Transport: Live audio and video streams are routed in real-time through LiveKit WebRTC infrastructure for high-speed low-latency peer connectivity.
  • No Automatic Video Archiving: CRINGE METER does not record, archive, or maintain 30-day recordings of standard video calls on its servers.
  • Safety Incident Moderation: If a user submits a Safety Report (via the in-match Report tool), the server records the reporting user ID, target user ID, match session identifier, timestamp, and reported reason for human and administrative review.
